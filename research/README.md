# Research record

Start with [methodology](methodology.md), [candidate register](candidate-register.md) and [coverage](coverage.md). The initial survey preceded the three Luna omission searches. Lead verification and independent Sol reviews followed those searches before the webpage was built.

The four *-entries.json files are research drafts, retained to show the initial findings. They include claims subsequently corrected or narrowed. They are not the published dataset. Use [the canonical records](../data/events.json), [factual findings](../reviews/facts.md), [review resolutions](../reviews/resolutions.md), and the [source ledger](source-ledger.md) for the final account.

The Luna notes record proposed additions and exclusions, not universal adoption judgments. Screened-only and deferred leads are not presented as fully researched. This distinction matters especially for ongoing or optional defenses.
