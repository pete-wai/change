# Change visual system

Mode: Read, with an interactive comparison exhibit. Code-led, newly created identity; the user authorized the webpage plan and its execution after research. No generated picture or incumbent comp governs this build.

## Direction

An illustrated editorial history, with the typographic confidence of a bound computing annual and the wayfinding of a reference book. Considered cultural references: computing annual; standards handbook; museum chronology; annotated technical journal; software changelog; circuit service manual; period browser chrome. The concept seed assigned the fourth, an annotated technical journal. Its large serif opening, margin dates, fine rules and factual before/after diagrams suit the researched material without imitating an old operating system.

Catalog challengers (seed f5d9be43) were declined in favor of product clarity: landscape posters encourage a scene with no historical evidence; pin-up calendars confuse editorial date ranges with calendar dates; tensegrity makes causal relationships look quantitative; doodle explainers imply a beginner tutorial; film strips impose horizontal navigation. Keep their discipline, respectively: a decisive ink palette, a stable time axis, explicitly labeled relationships, explanations next to their subject, and unambiguous selection state. User's approved autonomous implementation takes precedence over a new concept-choice round.

## First viewport and signature interaction

Masthead and compact site description precede a spacious two-column opening. A large serif heading sits alongside an exhibit of one concrete retired behavior. A native case selector and two state buttons drive the labeled flow diagram and explanation. The after-state visibly interrupts or changes the connection, while the case's source-linked record supplies historical scope. No autoplay, pretending to exploit software, or decorative terminal text.

Below: four era jumps, then filters and the chronology. Each row places the date and topic in a margin, followed by a title, short summary, links and native disclosure. Era boundaries get large typography and an overview. Expanded evidence uses a definition list and ordered milestone list. Details never require a modal.

## Responsive and interaction

At 800px the opening stacks. At 600px chronology dates become a compact row above the entry. Era jumps form two columns. Controls wrap; all targets are at least 44px tall. No horizontal timeline is required. Focus is a 3px teal outline with offset. Native details and links preserve keyboard and no-JS reading. Motion is limited to the exhibit state changing with a short opacity transition; reduced motion disables it.

## Reviewed implementation

The final exhibit uses a vertical route in all layouts. Blocked routes end before the destination; the permission case has a dashed continuation. It does not animate. The state control provides the comparison, and reduced-motion scrolling is immediate. The date rail uses short year ranges, with the longer chronology in details. Era titles were revised after review to “Windows gets new defaults” and “Changes you didn’t have to turn on.” The independent reviewer scored all four identified fixes resolved; see reviews/site-review.md. Browser tests cover reflow at 320px with doubled text.
